# Surah-Surah Prima

_The seven surahs where both the surah number AND ayah count are prime._

## The Seven Prime Surahs

| # | Surah | Number | Ayahs | nth Prime of Ayahs |
|---|-------|--------|-------|-------------------|
| 1 | Ar-Ra'd | 13 | 43 | 191 |
| 2 | Az-Zukhruf | 43 | 89 | 461 |
| 3 | Al-Qadr | 97 | 5 | 11 |
| 4 | Al-Qaari'a | 101 | 11 | 31 |
| 5 | Al-Asr | 103 | 3 | 5 |
| 6 | Al-Ma'un | 107 | 7 | 17 |
| 7 | Al-Falaq | 113 | 5 | 11 |

## Accumulation Calculation

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Sum of surah numbers | 577 |
| Sum of ayah counts | 163 |
| Sum of nth primes | 727 |
| Total count | 7 |

> **ALL NUMBERS ARE PRIME**: 577, 163, 727, and 7 are all prime numbers.

## Code

TypeScript implementation that:
1. Fetches all surah data from `api.alquran.cloud/v1/surah`
2. Filters surahs where both `number` and `numberOfAyahs` are prime
3. Maps to nth-prime values
4. Reduces to aggregate statistics
