# Angka 19 — Code 19

_The significance of the number 19 in mathematics and Quranic structure._

## Mathematical Properties

- **19 is the 8th prime number**
- Composed of 1 (first single-digit) and 9 (last single-digit)
- **Digital root property**: Any power of 19 reduces to 1:
  - 19¹ = 19 → 1+9 = 10 → 1+0 = **1**
  - 19² = 361 → 3+6+1 = 10 → **1**
  - 19³ = 6859 → 6+8+5+9 = 28 → 2+8 = 10 → **1**
  - 19⁴ = 130321 → 1+3+0+3+2+1 = 10 → **1**
  - 19⁷ = 893871739 → sum = 55 → 5+5 = 10 → **1**

## Quranic Significance

- **114 surahs = 6 × 19**
- The word **"Wahid"** (واحد, meaning "One") has an Abjad value of **19**
- 114th prime number is **619** (6-1-9 pattern)
- Bismillah's first letter "Baa" is said to contain the essence of the Quran

## Connection to Hisab Jummal

The word "Wahid" (One) = 19 in Abjad values, linking the concept of divine unity (Tawhid) with the number 19.
