# Prime Number Theory

_Fundamental properties and classification of prime numbers._

## Definition

A prime number is an integer > 1 divisible only by 1 and itself (exactly two factors).

**1 is neither prime nor composite** — it's called a **unit**.

## Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic

Every integer > 1 is either prime or can be uniquely factorized into primes (up to permutation).

## Analogy

> **Primes are atoms; composites are molecules.**

Just as every compound is made of atoms, every composite number is made of primes. The "chemical formula" of a number is its prime factorization.
