# Leonardo Fibonacci

_Italian mathematician (c. 1170–1250 CE), brought Hindu-Arabic numerals to Europe._

## Key Contribution

Published _Liber Abaci_ in 1202 CE after studying mathematics in North Africa. The book:
- Introduced Hindu-Arabic digits (0-9) to European merchants
- Demonstrated their superiority over Roman numerals for commerce
- Included the famous Fibonacci sequence (though this was originally an Indian mathematical concept)

## Role in Numeral History

Fibonacci was the final link in the transmission chain:
1. **India** → invented the numerals
2. **Al-Khwarizmi** → adapted them for Islamic world
3. **Fibonacci** → brought them to Europe
