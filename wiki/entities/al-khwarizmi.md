# Al-Khwarizmi

_Persian mathematician (c. 780–850 CE), father of algebra and bridge between Indian and Islamic mathematics._

## Key Contributions

- **825 CE**: Wrote _al-Jam' wa-l-tafriq bi-hisab al-Hind_ (Addition and Subtraction based on Hindu Calculation)
- Systematically adapted Indian numerals (including zero/"sifr") for the Arabic-speaking world
- His name → **Algorithm** (Latinized: Algoritmi)
- Also gave his name to **Algebra** (from _al-Jabr_, the title of his other major work)

## Role in Numeral History

Al-Khwarizmi was the critical link in the chain:
1. **India**: Invented the numerals and zero (Brahmagupta, Aryabhata)
2. **Al-Khwarizmi**: Adapted and systematized them for Islamic mathematics
3. **Europe**: Fibonacci brought them from North Africa to Italy
