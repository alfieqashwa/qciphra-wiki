# Vannevar Bush

_American engineer, science administrator, and visionary (1890–1974)._

## Key Contribution

Proposed the **[[memex]]** in his 1945 essay _"As We May Think"_ — a device for
storing and linking all of an individual's books, records, and communications,
with associative trails between documents.

## Relevance to LLM Wiki

Bush envisioned a personal, curated knowledge store where the connections between
documents are as valuable as the documents themselves. The part he couldn't solve
was **who does the maintenance**. The LLM Wiki pattern solves exactly that — the
LLM handles the bookkeeping that Bush's vision required but couldn't automate.
