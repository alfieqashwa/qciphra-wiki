# Digital Root

_The repeated sum of a number's digits until a single digit remains._

## Definition

The digital root of a number is obtained by repeatedly summing its digits until a single digit (1-9) is obtained.

Example: 6859 → 6+8+5+9 = 28 → 2+8 = 10 → 1+0 = **1**

## Connection to 19

The number **19 has a remarkable digital root property**: any power of 19 always reduces to **1**.

| Power | Value | Digital Root |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| 19¹ | 19 | 1 |
| 19² | 361 | 1 |
| 19³ | 6,859 | 1 |
| 19⁴ | 130,321 | 1 |
| 19⁷ | 893,871,739 | 1 |

This is because 19 ≡ 1 (mod 9), so 19ⁿ ≡ 1ⁿ ≡ 1 (mod 9).

## Significance

In the context of [[islamic-numerology]] and [[number-19]]:
- 19 is composed of 1 (first digit) and 9 (last digit)
- Its digital root always returning to 1 symbolizes divine unity (Tawhid)
- The word "Wahid" (One) has Abjad value 19 — the number that always reduces to One
