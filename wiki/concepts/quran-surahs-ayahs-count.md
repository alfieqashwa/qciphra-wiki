The Quran contains 114 chapters, known as Surahs.

The number of verses (Ayahs) varies slightly depending on different schools of counting, with the most commonly cited count being 6,236 verses (Kufic school). Other schools report counts such as 6,239, 6,225, 6,219, 6,211, and 6,204.

Additional statistics include approximately 77,430 total words (kalimahs), 290,883 letters (hurf), and a total Hisab Jummal of 23,283,600.
