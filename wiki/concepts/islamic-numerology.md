# Islamic Numerology (Hisab Jummal)

_The tradition of analyzing numerical patterns in the Quran using the Abjad letter-value system._

## Core System

Based on the **Abjad numeral system** ([[hisab-jummal]]) where each Arabic letter has a numeric value (1-1000). Words and phrases are analyzed by summing their letter values.

## Key Patterns in the Quran

### The 19-Code
- **114 surahs = 6 × 19**
- 114th prime = **619**
- "Wahid" (One) = **19** in Abjad → links divine unity (Tawhid) to the number 19
- Any power of 19 has digital root = **1** ([[digital-root]])

### Prime Number Patterns
- **7 surahs** have both prime surah numbers and prime ayah counts
- All accumulation values from these 7 surahs are themselves prime: 577, 163, 727, 7
- See: [[surah-surah-prima]]

### Parity Checksum
- **57:57** odd-even balance across surah+ayah sums
- Acts as structural integrity lock
- See: [[algoritma-checksum]]

### Asma Ul Husna Connections
- Name #19 (Al-'Alim) at position 19
- "Wahid" (One) = 19 in Abjad
- See: [[asma-ul-husna]]

## Historical Context

- Pre-Islamic Semitic tradition (shared with Jewish Gematria)
- Flourished during the Islamic Golden Age
- Used in astronomy, navigation, poetry, and metaphysics
- Modern computational tools enable systematic analysis at scale

## Academic Perspectives

**Supporting view**: The patterns are too precise and interlocking to be coincidental, especially the 57:57 parity and the all-prime accumulations.

**Critical view**: In large complex texts, some symmetric patterns are statistically inevitable. Results depend on which mushaf (Hafs vs Warsh) and which counting methodology is used.
