# Memex

_A hypothetical personal knowledge storage device proposed by [[vannevar-bush]] in
1945._

## Vision

A desk-sized device that would store all of an individual's books, records, and
communications, with **associative trails** linking related documents together.
Bush imagined the user creating named trails through their knowledge store —
clicking from one document to another via pre-built connections.

## Why It Matters

Bush's vision was closer to a personal wiki than to what the web became. The web
is public and passively consumed. The memex was private, actively curated, with
the connections between documents being as valuable as the documents themselves.

## The Missing Piece

Bush couldn't solve the **maintenance problem**. Who creates and maintains the
associative trails? In 1945, it had to be the human. In 2026, the LLM handles it.
