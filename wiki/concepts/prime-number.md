# Prime Numbers

_Integers > 1 divisible only by 1 and themselves — the "atoms" of number theory._

## Definition & Fundamental Theorem

A prime has exactly two factors: 1 and itself. **1 is a unit** (neither prime nor composite).

**Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic**: Every integer > 1 is either prime or can be uniquely factorized into primes.

## Analogy

> **Primes : Composites :: Atoms : Molecules**

Every composite number is a unique "molecule" of prime "atoms." The prime factorization is the number's "chemical formula."

## Classification

| Type | Code | Condition |
|------|------|-----------|
| Unit | U | The number 1 |
| Additive Prime | AP | Prime, and its digit sum is also prime |
| Non-additive Prime | XP | Prime, but digit sum is not prime |
| Additive Composite | AC | Composite, digit sum is composite |
| Non-additive Composite | XC | Composite, digit sum is not composite |

| **19** | 8th prime; 114 = 6×19; digital root always 1 |

## The Seven Prime Surahs

Seven surahs where **both** the surah number and ayah count are prime. Their accumulation totals (577, 163, 727, 7) are **all prime** — a remarkable convergence.
