# Zettelkasten

_A method of note-taking and knowledge management based on atomic, interlinked
notes._

## Core Principles

1. **Atomicity**: Each note captures one idea.
2. **Linking**: Notes are explicitly linked to related notes.
3. **Emergence**: Understanding emerges from the network of links, not from
   top-down organization.

## Connection to LLM Wiki

The Zettelkasten method shares the LLM Wiki's emphasis on linking and emergence.
The key difference: in a manual Zettelkasten, the human does all the linking and
maintenance. In the [[llm-wiki-pattern]], the LLM handles that automatically.
